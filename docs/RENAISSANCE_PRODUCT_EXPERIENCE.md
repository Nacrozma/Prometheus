# Prometheus 2.0: the product experience

Prometheus is one product with many environments (the constitution's "one identity, many distinct
experiences"). The identity: deep cosmic graphite, solar gold, champagne light, a living sun. The
environments, each with its own composition and interaction model (details: `RENAISSANCE_PAGE_SPECIFICATIONS.md`):

* **Launch** — the birth of the sun, then the app is there.
* **Home** — a living command centre: a welcome, an ask box, what needs you, where to continue, today.
* **Companion** — an immersive reading column with the sun's presence and state in words; approvals that
  explain themselves with real diffs.
* **Memory** — a library with an inspector and a review queue, and a map of how people, projects and
  topics connect.
* **Engineering** — a calculation studio and project intelligence.
* **Forge** — a mission studio: guided start, stages from real events, diffs, reports per discipline.
* **Workspace** — an explorer that is never a blank panel.
* **Reminders & tasks** — a planner with views, quick add and a checklist.
* **Obsidian** — a knowledge reader with links and backlinks.
* **Robot arm** — a lab studio, SIMULATION ONLY.
* **Reflection** — insights written as sentences, then charts with sample sizes.
* **Trust & safety** — an oversight timeline, data flows, permissions, undo.
* **Settings** — focused pages by intent; secure storage explained, never bypassed.
* **Search (Ctrl+K)** — grouped, highlighted, keyboard-first.

What it deliberately does not do: fake progress, show prices it cannot verify, imply hardware or
providers that are not there, or let any screen loosen a permission the core enforces.
